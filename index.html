How we are organised

One company, five divisions

Rather than a single service line, Mimperio runs as divisions that share engineering, a data provenance framework, and a privacy standard. Each serves a different need and time horizon.

DIV 01

Platforms

Flagship SaaS for socio-economic intelligence, forecasting, and coordinated delivery. For institutions that need continuous insight, not one-off reports.

GeoInsight · Foresight AI · PolicyFlow · DataGuard · PolicyLab · ModelForge
DIV 02

Applied AI Workflows

Task-focused AI that sits inside real workflows: summarising, drafting, cleaning data, flagging anomalies. Human in the loop, sources attached.

PolicyBrief AI · Research Copilot · Data-Cleanse Agent · Anomaly Sentinel · Nudge Personalizer · Knowledge Broker
DIV 03

Agile Tools

Lightweight, fast-to-deploy tools for a single job. Lower cost, quick to pilot, a practical on-ramp to the larger platforms.

LocalPulse · TrendSense · NudgeKit · PolicySim Lite · Contextualizer · PolicyConnect
DIV 04

Data Infrastructure & APIs

The plumbing: validation, cleaning, and a curated library of open models and datasets developers build on directly.

DataVerify · DataCleanse API · OpenEcon Hub
DIV 05

Vertical SaaS & Advisory

Sector products with trust-led distribution, plus the research arm, Meliora Nexus, that feeds fieldwork back into the platforms.

School Management System · Tax & Advisory Suite · Meliora Nexus
